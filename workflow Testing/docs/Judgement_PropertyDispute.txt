IN THE CIVIL COURT AT KOLKATA

Case No: CC/2026/45
Parties: Ram Kumar v. Kolkata Municipality

JUDGMENT:
The Court rules that the disputed property at 12B, Park Street, Kolkata,
rightfully belongs to Ram Kumar based on the registered title deeds.
Kolkata Municipality's claim is dismissed. Ram Kumar is granted possession
of the property.

Date: 19-Aug-2026
Judge: R. Chatterjee
