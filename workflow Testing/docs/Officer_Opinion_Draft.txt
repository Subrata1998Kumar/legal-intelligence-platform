Draft Opinion – Officer_1

Title: Withholding Payment Due to Delay

Content:
The vendor has failed to deliver the contracted reports within the stipulated
timeline. Under Clause 3 of the agreement, the department reserves the right
to withhold payment until satisfactory delivery is completed.

Risk Considerations:
- Possible breach of procurement requirements.
- The vendor may contest the withholding of payment, citing unforeseen circumstances.
- Advisor review and approval are required.
