Title: Withholding Payment Due to Delay

Content:
The vendor has failed to deliver the contracted reports within the stipulated timeline. 
As per clause 3 of the agreement, the department reserves the right to withhold payment 
until satisfactory delivery is made.

Risk Considerations:
- Possible breach of procurement compliance.
- Vendor may contest citing unforeseen circumstances.
- Requires advisor review and approval.
